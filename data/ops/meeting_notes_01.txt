THINK9 WEEKLY BRAND MEETING

Meeting Date: 2026-06-03
Category: Operations

Participants discussed the process for launching promotional campaigns.

The marketing team proposed creating a standard promotion checklist.

The checklist should include:
- Offer description
- Start and end dates
- Customer eligibility
- Product restrictions
- Required approvals
- Communication channels

The team agreed that campaign owners should complete the checklist before requesting final approval.

The goal is to reduce delays caused by missing information.
