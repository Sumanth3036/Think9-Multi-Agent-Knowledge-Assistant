NEW BRAND MANAGER ONBOARDING

New brand managers currently spend significant time asking existing team members about previous decisions.

The operations team recommends creating a searchable knowledge base containing previous campaign decisions, operating procedures, and important meeting outcomes.

The knowledge base should always identify the source and date of information.

If information conflicts between documents, the newest authoritative document should normally be preferred, subject to human review when the issue is significant.
