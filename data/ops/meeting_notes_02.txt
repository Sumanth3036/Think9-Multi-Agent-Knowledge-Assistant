THINK9 BRAND OPERATIONS MEETING

Meeting Date: 2026-06-17
Category: Operations

The team discussed repeated questions from newly assigned brand managers.

Several managers were unable to find previous decisions because information was stored across different documents.

The team proposed creating a centralized decision history.

The decision history should record:
- The original question
- The decision
- The reason for the decision
- The responsible team
- The date

The group agreed that future important decisions should be recorded in a common system.
