THINK9 LEGAL PLAYBOOK
Document Date: 2026-05-15
Category: Legal
Applies To: Consumer Brands

PROMOTIONAL DISCOUNTS

Two-for-one promotions are permitted only when the promotional terms are clearly displayed to customers.

The offer must clearly state:
- The products included in the promotion.
- The promotional period.
- Any quantity limitations.
- Whether the offer applies to new or existing customers.

A previous campaign review identified a legal concern when a two-for-one promotion did not clearly communicate quantity restrictions.

For promotions involving regulated products or unclear customer eligibility, legal review is required before launch.

MARKETING CLAIMS

Marketing material must not make unsupported performance claims.

Claims such as "best", "number one", "guaranteed results", or similar statements require supporting evidence before publication.

If evidence is unavailable, the marketing claim should be revised or submitted to legal for review.

CUSTOMER TERMS

Promotional terms must be consistent across advertising channels.

If the website, social media advertisement, and point-of-sale material contain conflicting terms, the promotion must be reviewed before launch.

LEGAL REVIEW RULE

When there is uncertainty about whether a campaign complies with this playbook, the final decision must be reviewed by a human member of the legal team.
